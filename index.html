Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need technical knowledge to use this? +
Not at all. We handle everything — from building the bot to connecting it to your WhatsApp number. You just tell us about your business and we take care of the rest.
What makes this an AI bot — not a regular chatbot? +
Regular chatbots only respond to specific keywords or button clicks. Our Genius Bot uses real AI to understand natural language — so when a customer types "do you have the blue one in a bigger size?", the bot actually understands what they mean and replies intelligently.
Can the bot take orders? +
Yes. The bot can collect order details — product, size, quantity, delivery address, and payment method — and send them directly to you. It works for fashion, food, real estate and most other businesses.
How do customers access the bot? +
Customers message your existing WhatsApp business number. Nothing changes for them — they just get instant, intelligent replies instead of waiting for you to reply manually.
Is there a monthly fee? +
No. You pay a one-time setup fee and your bot runs indefinitely at no extra cost. You only pay again if you want updates or changes — and even those are charged per task, not per month.
What if I want changes after delivery? +
Minor edits are free for the first 7 days after delivery. After that, we charge per task — adding new products is ₦5,000, editing messages is ₦8,000, and so on. No surprise monthly bills.
Do you serve businesses outside Lagos? +
Yes. We serve businesses across all of Nigeria. Everything is done remotely — you don't need to be in Lagos or meet us in person.
