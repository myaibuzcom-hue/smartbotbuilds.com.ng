The one-step rule

Most beginners paste a big idea into an AI chat and get a wall of code they cannot follow. The fix is simple: the AI gives you one step, you do it, you report back, and only then do you get the next one.

Example of how a session feels (illustrative)
  1. Describe your idea in plain words using Template 1. The AI turns it into a proper build brief.
  2. Paste the Executioner Rule (Template 2) at the end of that message. It tells the AI to act as your step-by-step partner.
  3. Do the first step, then reply DONE, or paste the exact error you got. Repeat.
  4. Before the chat gets long, use Template 3 to carry everything into a fresh chat without losing your progress.

Your three templates

Tap Copy, paste into any AI chat, and replace the parts in square brackets with your own details.

Template 1: The idea brief

Use this first. It makes the AI plan before it builds.

I want to build: [describe your idea in plain words].
Who it is for: [who will use it].
The one problem it solves: [one sentence].
My skill level: I do not write code. I can copy, paste and run commands.
My computer: [Windows, Mac or phone], with [tools you have installed].

Before you answer, turn this into a full build brief. List every feature a good first version needs, what could go wrong, and the security risks (never trust the user's browser, never put secrets in code). Then list what should wait for version 2. Do not write code yet.

Template 2: The Executioner Rule

Paste this at the end of your brief. It is what keeps the AI from overwhelming you.

THE EXECUTIONER RULE
You are my step-by-step coding partner. I do not write code. I only carry out your instructions and report back.
1. Give me ONE step at a time. Never more than one.
2. Zero assumptions. Tell me exactly what to open, click, name, paste and run.
3. Give complete code only: the full file, its exact path, and whether it is NEW or a REPLACEMENT. No placeholders and no "add your code here".
4. After every step, tell me what I should see, then stop and wait.
5. When I reply DONE, give the next step. If I send an error, fix that error first.
6. If anything could delete data, cost money or expose a secret, warn me and wait for my approval.
Start with Step 1.

When something fails, send the AI the full error text and a screenshot. Do not describe it from memory.

Template 3: The handoff request

Use this when a chat gets long or slow. Paste the result into a new chat and carry on.

This chat is getting long. Write a handoff prompt I can paste into a new chat. Include: what we are building, what is finished and tested, where we stopped, the exact next step, the folder and file list, the decisions we made and why, any errors still open, and the rules from the Executioner Rule. Do not include passwords or keys.

Questions people ask

Do I need to pay for an AI tool?

These prompts work in any AI chat. Many have free plans, but their limits and rules change, so check the one you use.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You need to be able to copy, paste, follow one instruction at a time and report exactly what you see.

Will this guarantee I finish a product?

No. The method makes the process clearer and easier to recover when something breaks. Results depend on your idea, your effort and the tools you use.

Is AI-written code safe to use with real money or customer data?

It needs to be reviewed first. The playbook includes a security checklist, but nothing replaces a proper review before real users and real money are involved.

What is the difference between this kit and the playbook?

The kit gives you the three core prompts. The playbook adds the master prompts, the security checklist, the error-reporting templates and the handoff prompts for building a full product.
