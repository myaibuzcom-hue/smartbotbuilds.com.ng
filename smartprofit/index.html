Welcome to SmartProfit

The easiest way to track your real profit, manage stock, and track debts without stress. Let's take a quick tour.

📦

1. Add Your Stock

Before you can make sales, add your products in the Stock tab. Include the cost price, selling price, and quantity.

🛒

2. Make Sales in Seconds

In the Quick Sale tab, tap a product or scan a barcode. If it's a cash sale, it auto-prints a receipt. If credit, it logs to the Debt Book.

📊

3. Watch Your Profit

Your dashboard shows exactly how much cash you have, what you are owed, and a chart of your daily profit. Ready to start?
