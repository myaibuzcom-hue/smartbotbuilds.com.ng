I started SmartBot Builds because I saw too many Nigerian businesses losing customers simply because they couldn't reply in time. Friends who run fashion stores, restaurants, and service businesses would wake up to dozens of missed WhatsApp messages — and missed sales.

💬 "Every unanswered message is a lost sale. I wanted to fix that — affordably, and without monthly fees."

With a background in software development and a deep understanding of the Nigerian market, I build AI bots that understand local context, speak local languages, and work 24/7. And for businesses that also need a website, I deliver professional sites with free hosting — because being online shouldn't be expensive.
