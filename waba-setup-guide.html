Simple Setup Guide

Choose one of the two options below. We will guide you step by step.

Option A: Do It Yourself (6 Steps)

Click to see the detailed step-by-step guide

  1. Create a Meta Business Account
    1. Open your web browser and go to business.facebook.com/overview.
    2. If you see a login button, log in with your Facebook account.
    3. Click "Create account".
    4. Enter your business name (or personal name) and your email address.
    5. Click Submit.
    6. Go to your email inbox or spam folder, find the confirmation email from Meta, and click the confirmation link.
  2. Create a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)
    1. After confirming your email, you'll be inside Meta Business Suite.
    2. Look at the left menu. If you don't see the menu, click the three horizontal lines (hamburger) at the top left.
    3. Scroll down and click Settings (gear icon).
    4. Under the Accounts section, click WhatsApp Accounts.
    5. Click the blue Add button, then select Create a new WhatsApp Business Account.
    6. Enter a name (your business name), select your time zone (for Nigeria choose (GMT+01:00) West Central Africa), and click Create.
  3. Add Your Dedicated Bot Number
    1. Click on your new WABA name.
    2. Look for Phone numbers and click Add phone number.
    3. In the phone number field, enter the number you want to use for the bot. Start with +234 and remove the leading zero. Example: if the number is 09064586135, enter +2349064586135.
    4. For the display name, enter your business name.
    5. Choose SMS or Voice call for verification.
    6. Click Next or Continue.
    7. Meta will send a 6-digit code to that number via SMS or call. The code will arrive on the phone where the SIM is inserted.
    8. Enter that code in the field and click Verify.
  4. Generate a Permanent Access Token
    1. Still in Meta Business Suite, click Settings (gear icon) again.
    2. Under Accounts, click System users.
    3. Click the blue Add button and choose Create system user.
    4. In the form, type SmartBot Integration as the system user name, and set role to Admin.
    5. Click Create.
    6. Find the new system user in the list and click on its name.
    7. Click Add assets, select WhatsApp Accounts, check the box next to your WABA, and turn on Manage access. Click Save.
    8. Now click Generate token.
    9. Select whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management.
    10. Click Generate.
    11. Copy the long token that appears. Keep it secret and send it to us via WhatsApp.
  5. Set Webhook URL
    1. Go back to Settings → WhatsApp Accounts.
    2. Click on your WABA name.
    3. Find the Configuration or Webhooks section.
    4. Click Edit.
    5. In the Callback URL field, type exactly: https://smartbot-genius-bot.myaibuzcom-hue.deno.net
    6. In the Verify token field, type exactly: smartbot
    7. Click Verify and Save. You should see a green success message.
    8. If asked to subscribe to fields, make sure messages is checked.
  6. Get Your Phone Number ID and Send to Us
    1. Go back to Settings → WhatsApp Accounts → your WABA → Phone numbers.
    2. Find your number. Next to it is the Phone Number ID.
    3. Copy the Phone Number ID.
    4. Send the Phone Number ID and the Access Token to us via WhatsApp. We will activate your bot.
✅ This DIY route takes about 20 minutes. If you prefer not to do these steps, use Option B and we'll handle it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an App?

No. That's an old step. We handle app creation if needed.

Do I need to create a System User?

Only if you choose Option A (Do It Yourself). If you choose Option B, we create it for you.

What is a Phone Number ID?

It's a unique number Meta gives after you add your bot number to your WABA. We use it to identify your bot.

Can I use my personal WhatsApp number?

No. The bot needs a dedicated SIM that is not already on WhatsApp. Buy a new SIM (₦200) if needed.
