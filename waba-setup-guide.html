WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)
Setup Guide

Follow these steps carefully to create your own WhatsApp Business Account, connect your bot number, and get your Phone Number ID. Once complete, return to the form and submit your details.

1 Create a Meta Business Account

  1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Safari).
  2. In the address bar, type exactly: business.facebook.com/overview and press Enter.
  3. If you are not logged into Facebook, log in with your personal Facebook account.
  4. If you are logged in but don't have a business account, click "Create an account".
  5. Enter your business name. If you don't have a registered business, enter your personal name (e.g., "Chioma Okafor").
  6. Enter your email address (the one you check regularly).
  7. Click Submit.
  8. Go to your email inbox (or spam folder). Find an email from Meta/Facebook with a confirmation link. Click that link.
  9. You are now logged into Meta Business Suite.
💡 If you already have a Meta Business Account, skip this step and log in.

2 Create a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)

  1. In Meta Business Suite, look at the left side of the screen. If you don't see a menu, click the three horizontal lines (hamburger icon) at the top left.
  2. Scroll down in the left menu and find "Settings" (gear icon). Click it.
  3. In the Settings page, look for a section called "Accounts". Under Accounts, click "WhatsApp Accounts".
  4. You will see a blue button that says "Add". Click it.
  5. A dropdown menu appears. Click "Create a new WhatsApp Business Account".
  6. Fill in:
    • Account name: Enter your business name (or your name).
    • Time zone: Select your time zone. For Nigeria, choose (GMT+01:00) West Central Africa.
  7. Click Create.
  8. You will see your new WhatsApp Business Account listed on the page. Click on its name to open it.

3 Add a Dedicated Bot Number

  1. Inside your WABA page, look for a section called "Phone numbers".
  2. Click the blue "Add phone number" button.
  3. A form appears:
    • Phone number: Enter the dedicated SIM number you prepared. Start with +234 and remove the first zero. Example: if the number is 07069593730, type +2347069593730.
    • Display name: Enter your business name as it should appear to customers.
  4. Choose "SMS" or "Voice call" as the verification method.
  5. Click Next or Continue.
  6. Meta will send a 6-digit code to that number via SMS or call. The code will arrive on the phone where the SIM is inserted.
  7. Type that code into the field on the screen.
  8. Click Verify.
  9. After verification, Meta will show a "Phone Number ID" – a long number. It looks like 123456789012345.
  10. Copy this Phone Number ID and keep it safe. You will need it later.
⚠️ If the number is already on WhatsApp, you must delete that WhatsApp account first (or use a new SIM). See the FAQ below.

4 Generate a Permanent Access Token

  1. Still in Meta Business Suite, click "Settings" (gear icon) in the left menu again.
  2. Under "Accounts", click "System users".
  3. Click the blue "Add" button → "Create system user".
  4. In the form:
    • System user name: Type SmartBot Integration.
    • Role: Choose Admin.
  5. Click Create.
  6. You will see the new system user in a list. Click on its name.
  7. Click "Add assets".
  8. A window opens. Select "WhatsApp Accounts".
  9. Check the box next to your WABA.
  10. Turn on "Manage" access by toggling the switch.
  11. Click Save.
  12. Now click "Generate token".
  13. Select the permissions:
    • whatsapp_business_messaging
    • whatsapp_business_management
  14. Click Generate.
  15. Copy the long token that appears. Keep it secret. Do not send it to anyone.
  16. Click Done.

5 Set Webhook URL

  1. In Meta Business Suite, go back to Settings → WhatsApp Accounts.
  2. Click on your WABA name.
  3. Look for a tab or section called "Configuration" or "Webhooks". It may be under "Webhook fields".
  4. Click Edit.
  5. In the Callback URL field, type exactly:
    https://smartbot-genius-bot.myaibuzcom-hue.deno.net
  6. In the Verify token field, type exactly:
    smartbot
  7. Click Verify and Save.
  8. You should see a green success message. If you get an error, double-check the URL and token spelling.
  9. After saving, subscribe to the messages field if you see a list of fields.
🔒 The callback URL is safe to share. It is not a secret, and it is protected by the verify token.

6 Get Your Phone Number ID (Again)

  1. Go back to Settings → WhatsApp Accounts → your WABA → Phone numbers.
  2. Find the number you added. Next to it, you'll see the Phone Number ID.
  3. Copy that ID.

✅ You're Done!

Now go back to the form you were filling and submit it with your Phone Number ID. Segun will activate your bot within hours.

❓ FAQ

My number is already on WhatsApp. What do I do?

You have two options:

  • Option A: Delete the existing WhatsApp account on that number (Settings → Account → Delete My Account). Then add it again in your WABA.
  • Option B: Buy a new SIM (any network, ~₦200) and use that new number for the bot.

What is a Phone Number ID?

It's a unique number Meta gives after you add your bot number to your WABA. It lets our system identify your bot and apply your business's training.

Do I need to send my token to SmartBot Builds?

No. The token is secret. Keep it to yourself. We only need the Phone Number ID.

Is the callback URL safe to share?

Yes. The URL is not a secret. It's protected by the verify token, and only Meta can send valid events to it.
