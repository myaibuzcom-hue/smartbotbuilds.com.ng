Questions

Things school owners ask us

Who owns our school's data?

Your school does. Every database table is protected by row-level security, so a parent can never see another parent's child, and your data is backed up automatically every day. You can cancel anytime, and your data is always yours.

Do our teachers need laptops?

No. Score entry is mobile-first and works on any Android phone. Admins can use a phone or a computer.

Can parents see results before we approve them?

No. Teachers submit scores, the admin reviews and approves them, and nothing reaches parents until the admin publishes.

What if a teacher changes a score after results are published?

Published results keep a snapshot of the exact scores at publish time, and every sensitive action is recorded in the audit log with who did it and when.

Who types in our students and staff?

For the first 5 founder schools, we import all your students and staff from your Excel sheets. You don't type a single name.

How does pricing work?

You pay a one-time setup fee, then a flat fee per term that covers hosting, daily backups, updates and support. It doesn't grow when you add students.

Can I see it before I decide?

Yes. The live demo has no signup and no obligation, and it is view-only. Or book a 20-minute walkthrough and we will show you the system on your own school's structure.
