Why SmartSchool NG

From Spreadsheets & Paper to a Real System

Most Nigerian schools run on handwritten registers and Excel files. SmartSchool NG replaces all of it with a secure, browser-based system your staff can use from any device.

❌ Without SmartSchool NG

  • Results compiled by hand in Excel — errors everywhere
  • Parents call the office repeatedly for updates
  • Report cards printed on paper and picked up in person
  • No record of who changed what, when, or why
  • Teachers carry bulky mark books between classes
  • Approval chains break down silently
  • Lost files, no backups, no way to recover

✅ With SmartSchool NG

  • Teachers enter scores on their phones, in seconds
  • Admins approve results with one click
  • Parents log in and see published results instantly
  • Every action is logged with the user's identity
  • Report cards download as clean PDFs with signatures
  • Full workflow: draft → submitted → approved → published
  • Data lives in a secure, backed-up cloud database

How Results Flow

A Safe Workflow from Scores to Parents

Nothing reaches parents until the admin publishes it. Every step is logged.

1

Draft

Teacher enters scores for their subject

2

Submitted

Batch sent to admin for review

3

Approved

Admin reviews and approves the batch

4

Published

Parents can now see results in their portal

Features

Everything a Nigerian School Actually Needs

No bloat. No features you'll never use. Just the tools that make your school run smoothly.

📊

Flexible Rubrics

CA1, CA2, Assignments, and Exams — configure weights per class or per subject.

🎯

Nigerian Grading

Standard 75–100 A to 0–39 F scale, applied consistently across subjects and terms.

📅

Attendance Tracking

Class teachers record daily attendance. It appears on the report card.

🤖

AI Comment Assist

Teachers and principals get optional AI suggestions for student comments — editable before saving.

✍️

Digital Signatures

Upload the principal's and class teachers' signatures once. They appear automatically on every PDF.

🖼️

School Branding

Upload your school logo — it appears at the top of every report card, automatically.

🔒

Row-Level Security

Every database table is locked down. A parent can never see another parent's child.

📜

Audit Trail

Every sensitive action — approvals, publishes, edits, reopens — is logged with who and when.

📄

Historical Integrity

Result snapshots preserve the exact score at publish time, even if a teacher edits later.
